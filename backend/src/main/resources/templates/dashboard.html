FAQ

What is Prime Vehicle Rentals? +
Prime Vehicle Rentals is Sri Lanka's premier luxury rental car service offering free delivery and pickup across Colombo, Kandy, Galle, and major cities.
What luxury vehicles do you offer? +
Our fleet includes a range of luxury and executive vehicles — all well-maintained and latest models. Browse our fleet to see what's available.
Do you deliver rental cars? +
Yes, free delivery within Colombo, Kandy and Galle metro areas between 8am–8pm.
How can I pick up a rental car? +
Free delivery to your location or pickup from our garage at No. 48, Galle Road, Colombo 03.
What payment methods are accepted? +
We accept Cash, Card, and Online payments in LKR.
Can I rent for a one-way trip? +
Yes, one-way rentals are available. Contact us for details.
